Hi, I'm Shujie Zhang

I am currently a first-year Ph.D. student in a joint training program between Shanghai AI Lab and the School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Kailin Li, Weinan Zhang, and Xiaokang Yang.

Before that, I received my B.Eng. from the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University in 2026 and worked as a research intern at Shanghai AI Lab, advised by Jiangmiao Pang and Xudong Xu. During my undergraduate time, I also had the good fortune to work at BIGAI with Baoxiong Jia and Yandan Yang.
